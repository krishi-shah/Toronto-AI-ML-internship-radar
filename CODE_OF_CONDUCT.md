# Code of Conduct

This project follows the spirit of the
[Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/).
It is a small project made by and for students looking for work. Please keep
it a place where anyone can ask a question or open a pull request without
feeling unwelcome.

## Expected

- Be kind and patient, especially with people contributing for the first time.
- Assume good intent; ask before assuming.
- Give feedback about code and ideas, never about the person.
- Respect people's time: keep issues focused and include what you tried.

## Not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, or discriminatory remarks of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, or sustained disruption of discussion.
- Publishing anyone's private information without their permission.
- Using the issue tracker to advertise, recruit, or spam.

## Reporting

If someone's behaviour concerns you, contact the maintainer,
[@krishi-shah](https://github.com/krishi-shah), privately through GitHub. Reports are
handled confidentially. The maintainer may edit or remove comments, close
issues, or block contributors who break this code.
